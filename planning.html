THE EYLF V2.0 PLANNING CYCLE

COMPONENT 04

Promoting holistic development and wellbeing

Holistic development recognises that cognitive, physical, social, emotional, linguistic, spiritual and creative learning are interconnected. The planning cycle helps educators make informed decisions from careful observation and ongoing reflection.

✿
A CONTINUOUS CYCLE

From noticing to evaluating

Select each stage to explore how evidence of children’s learning informs responsive curriculum decisions.

STAGE 01

Observe

Gather authentic documentation such as anecdotal observations, learning stories, photographs and family insights. Notice pre-verbal gestures, play schemas, interests and physiological rhythms without interrupting deep engagement.

EVIDENCE-INFORMED PRACTICE

Play, communication and partnership

Communication and language

Responsive, language-rich interactions create opportunities for shared attention, vocabulary, gesture, listening and reciprocal communication.

Intentional interaction

Family engagement

Families contribute essential knowledge about children’s strengths, interests, culture, language, routines and changing needs.

Shared understanding

Play-based learning

Play creates meaningful contexts for exploration and learning. Observation and reflection help educators extend experiences without taking over.

Child-led inquiry

Documentation should make learning visible

Record what the child did and communicated, interpret the learning carefully, connect it to the EYLF, and identify a realistic next step. Avoid treating milestones as a checklist or assuming that one observation tells the whole story.
