NQS QUALITY AREA 3 · SAFE, INCLUSIVE & RESPONSIVE SPACES

COMPONENT 03

Infant and toddler play environments

An effective environment is more than a room arrangement. It is a carefully considered learning partner that supports safe movement, sensory exploration, relationships, rest, agency and connection with the natural world.

✿
DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Spaces that invite discovery

👁️

Clear sightlines

Arrange spaces so educators can supervise children while allowing infants and toddlers to explore independently.

🧺

Accessible materials

Use low, stable shelving and a thoughtful selection of open-ended, age-appropriate resources.

🌳

Nature and calm

Include natural textures, outdoor experiences, quiet retreats and spaces that reduce unnecessary sensory overload.

ENVIRONMENT ZONES

Indoor and outdoor spaces

Choose a zone to explore its purpose and pedagogical considerations.

🌙

Sleep sanctuary

Provide a calm, appropriately equipped sleep area with clear supervision and safe-rest practices. Follow current Australian safe-sleep guidance, individual plans and service procedures.

Safe rest · Regulation 84A and applicable requirements
🧼

Care and nappy hub

Maintain hygienic facilities, accessible handwashing and safe change arrangements. Make care routines private, respectful and relational.

Hygiene · Dignity · Responsive care
🪵

Discovery floor

Offer open floor space, low shelving, stable furniture and rotated heuristic materials that invite independent investigation.

Movement · Agency · Play-based learning
☁️

Quiet retreat nook

Include soft furnishings, board books and a quieter place to pause, regulate and reconnect with a trusted educator.

Co-regulation · Inclusion · Rest
🍃

Biophilic outdoor area

Provide natural shade, accessible planting, grass, water play and opportunities to notice seasonal change and care for living things.

Sustainability · Science · Connection to Country
🪨

Calibrated motor trail

Offer low, stable challenges such as gentle ramps and varied surfaces, with close supervision and individual risk-benefit assessment.

Balance · Coordination · Physical confidence
REGULATORY ALIGNMENT

Quality Area 3 in practice

NQS Standard 3.1 — Design: The environment is fit for purpose and maintained in a safe, clean condition.

NQS Standard 3.2 — Use: The environment supports access and participation, play-based learning and sustainability.

Related considerations: National Regulations, equipment and product safety, supervision, safe sleep, hygiene, inclusion and service risk assessments.

Always verify regulatory references, current Australian Standards and safe-sleep requirements against the latest ACECQA guidance, relevant state or territory requirements, and your service policies before implementing an environment design.
