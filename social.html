Fostering social development

HASS · DRAMA · PUPPETRY
EDUCATOR PRACTICE

Teaching competencies

✦

Educators model warm interactions, interpret peer intentions aloud, support communication and mediate conflict without forcing sharing. They create small-group opportunities and value family languages, identities and cultural practices.

AUTHENTIC CURRICULUM

Learning across curriculum areas

Humanities and Social Sciences: explore family photographs and community experiences. Drama and puppetry: use soft puppets to practise greetings, gentle interactions and caring for others. Invite family contributions and avoid assumptions about family structures.

ORIGINAL LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES

Three experiences, thoughtfully adapted by age

Each experience is designed around the child’s stage, interests, cues and individual capabilities.

0–12 months

Mirror Mat Peer Greetings

Nurture social noticing, shared attention and warm peer awareness.

Position a safe, securely fixed mirror at floor level beside a firm mat. Sit at infant eye level and gently name expressions, sounds and movements. Follow the infant’s attention and avoid overstimulating the interaction.

EYLF Outcome 1 · Safe relationships
12–24 months

Rolling Ball Turn-Taking Circle

Support simple reciprocal peer exchange and early turn-taking.

Offer soft, easy-to-grasp balls on a clear cushioned area. Model rolling a ball to a peer and narrate the exchange. Follow children’s cues; do not require a child to give up a toy before they are ready.

EYLF Outcomes 1 & 2 · Empathy
2–3 years

Community Picnic Role-Play

Practise shared play, role negotiation and cultural inclusion.

Provide a picnic rug, safe play utensils and soft puppets. Invite children to plan a pretend picnic together and include greetings, foods and family experiences contributed by families.

EYLF Outcomes 1 & 2 · Diversity
NOMINATED LEARNING MATERIAL

Baby Business (Seymour, 2019)

BOOK

The story depicts a Darug baby being welcomed to Country through a smoking ceremony. It introduces kinship, belonging, Darug language and connection to Country.

Video Recording 2 · Picture book reading

Use the material respectfully and adapt your delivery to children’s ages, interests, language backgrounds and responses.

Practice reminder

Learning experiences must be voluntary, closely supervised and adapted to each child. Check every material for age suitability, choking hazards, allergens, hygiene and other risks before use. Follow service policies and family information.
